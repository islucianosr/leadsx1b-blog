Welcome to Leadsx1B! This step-by-step onboarding walkthrough gets your outbound revenue engine up and running in under 15 minutes without writing a single line of code.

---

## 1. Creating Your First Extraction Task

From your Leadsx1B dashboard, navigate to **"New Search"**. Enter your target industry (e.g., *"Roofing Contractors"* or *"Digital Marketing Agencies"*) and select your geographic market.

---

## 2. Enabling AI Enrichment and WhatsApp Verification

Turn on **"Verify WhatsApp"** and **"Scrape Company Email"**. Leadsx1B scans the business site and live phone carriers to ensure each prospect is directly reachable.

---

## 3. Launching Your Outreach via Built-in WhatsApp & CRM

Move qualified leads to your **Outreach** column and trigger your personalized template sequence using `{firstName}` and `{companyName}` dynamic tags.
