## Understanding Meta's Anti-Spam Detection Engine

To prevent bans, you must understand how Meta's automated surveillance models flag accounts:

1. **User Block & Report Rate:** This is the single highest-weighted factor. If more than 3% of recipients click *"Block and Report Spam"*, your number enters automatic probation or immediate shutdown.
2. **Robotic Velocity Patterns:** Firing 50 messages per minute with identical text triggers heuristic alarms instantly.
3. **Number Age & Trust Score:** A SIM card activated yesterday has a near-zero trust score. A phone number with 6+ months of natural bidirectional conversations enjoys wide leeway.
4. **Lack of Incoming Conversations:** Spammer accounts send 1,000 messages and receive 2 replies. High-reputation accounts have balanced back-and-forth communication.

## The F.A.C.T. High-Converting WhatsApp Framework

Nobody reads wall-of-text sales pitches on their personal phone screen. Successful cold messages must feel human, concise, and effortless to reply to.

Use the **F.A.C.T.** formula:

### 1. F - Familiarity
State clearly how you found them (Google Maps profile, local business directory, or industry reference).

### 2. A - Specific Analysis
Highlight a verifiable, positive detail about their business. For example: *"I noticed your dental clinic on Google Maps has over 150 5-star reviews."*

### 3. C - Curiosity & Value Hook
Provide an intriguing observation without pitching features. Sell curiosity, not the product.

### 4. T - Low-Friction Transition
Ask a binary or low-friction question (*"Are you open to seeing a 60-second video of how other clinics in [City] are doing this?"*).
