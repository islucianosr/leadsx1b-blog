## The 5 Crucial Stages of a Lean B2B Pipeline

Simplicity beats complexity every time. Focus on clean, objective pipeline stages:

1. **New Leads Ingested:** Google Maps leads enriched with phone and verified WhatsApp.
2. **Contact Initiated:** First outbound WhatsApp template delivered.
3. **Discovery Call Booked:** Meeting confirmed with decision maker.
4. **Proposal In Review:** Contract or proposal sent.
5. **Closed Won:** Agreement finalized.
